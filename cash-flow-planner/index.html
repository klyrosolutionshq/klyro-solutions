05 / FAQ

Before you download

Is this a spreadsheet that calculates for me?

No. This is a printable 16-page PDF workbook to fill in by hand, or with a tablet's PDF markup app. It does not automatically calculate or connect to bank accounts. For an Excel invoice and fee calculator, see TakeHome Sheet.

Does it work with any currency?

You record planning totals in your home currency and enter invoice currencies, exchange-rate assumptions and fees yourself. Rates in the example are illustrative.

How much time does it take?

The workbook suggests about 30 minutes to complete the initial seven steps, weekly check-ins, and a 10-minute month-end review.

Can I print on US Letter paper?

Yes. The pages are designed for A4; select “fit to page” when printing on US Letter.

Does it tell me what taxes I owe?

No. You choose and verify the tax set-aside percentage using an accountant or relevant tax-authority guidance. The planner is not financial, legal, tax or accounting advice.

Can I share the PDF?

The product is licensed for personal or single-business use, not sharing, redistribution or resale.
